# Rail Arena concept artwork

These two supporting figures are resized, JPEG-compressed copies of already-public Rail Arena concept artwork. Their full frames and existing labels are preserved; no new image generation, crop or subject alteration was applied.

Source repository: [Evekiio/rail-arena](https://github.com/Evekiio/rail-arena), commit `f805fbd32129999955c31fa659e5ea0092102ba5`.

The public package manifest identifies built-in image_gen and the approved source. This is concept artwork, not a render of installed meshes or gameplay. |
